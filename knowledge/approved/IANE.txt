Sign-in/Signout?

Sign-in and Sign-out must be done
on your work system ONLY

Don't do it on your mobile

Working Hours

You must maintain an effective total of 9.5 working hours
daily (Monday to Friday). This includes a 30-minute
lunch/dinner and a 30-minute evening break.

Any employee who works less than 5 hours is considered on
leave, and anyone who works more than 5 hours, but less
than 8 hours is considered on half-day leave.

Break Time Policy

Break Notification:
If you are stepping away from your workspace for 15 minutes or more,
it is mandatory to post a break message in the official team group
indicating your unavailability.
This policy is intended to maintain transparency, accountability, and
effective collaboration within the team. Your adherence to these
guidelines is appreciated.

Coming late to work?

Inform your Manager in advance if you are coming late to
work for more than 30 minutes

Leaving early?

If you need to leave early (early sign-out), please ensure you
get approval from both your Team Leader (TL) and
Manager at least a day in advance.

To maintain our productivity standards, we are required to
complete 8.5 hours of productive work and a 10-hour shift
each day.

If you need to sign out early, please make sure to sign in
earlier that day to balance your shift hours accordingly.

Permission

Permission can only be granted for up to 1.5 hours.

If the time exceeds 1.5 hours, it will be counted as Half a Day
Leave.

Permission cannot be granted on the same day. The
approval should be received via email.

Mobile phone usage

No personal calls/work is allowed during office hours

Your personal phone must be kept in silent mode

Share our office main number to your family so they can
reach you if in case of any emergencies during your office
hours.

Planning leave?

You must talk to your manager first and get his approval priorly
before 2 weeks.

Once approved send an email to your manager, CC to your team
lead and HR in the defined format

Do not plan your leave/travel until unless you receive email
confirmation from your lead

Be informed/prepared that your leave may get cancelled even
after approval sometimes due to unexpected client calls or
workload.

Sudden leaves are not encouraged and not appreciated and it
may result in holding your salary.

Leaves

Every employee is eligible for 18 paid leaves during the year
(21st January to 20th January). i.e. 12 casual leaves and 6
sick leaves but only after they are accrued.

Planning leaves for more than a day? Always discuss with
your manager first before planning.

Always get your leaves planned and approved priorly by 2
weeks.

Leaves encashment

Employees are eligible to encash (up to 12 days in a year), each day of
balance leave at the rate of one day’s latest gross salary.

Balance leaves accrued between January 21st of
the previous year, and January 20th of the current year will be
encashed 100% if you are on active payroll till the end of January.

If the employee is leaving the organisation before completion
of full leave calendar year, then they will be paid half day gross salary
for each balance leave day.

Leave application

Leave application must be sent to your manager by sending email in
defined format. Check with HR about the format.

No leaves should be taken in the first 60 days of employment.

Subject line should be
Employee name (Employee ID) - Leave application

Payments

Salary will be paid 1st of every month

Salary calculated from 21st of Jan to 20th of Feb (30 days) and will be
continued for the year.

TDS is applicable as per the tax slab – Government Norms

For employees who choose to have dinner at the office, the cost will
be shared between the employee and the company. The employee’s
share will be deducted from their salary as a standard deduction.

Salary will be put hold if any leaves taken during the first 60 days.
Please note that the company has the right to take a action on salary
disbursements.

Holiday payout

If you prefer to work on any holiday, you will be given two options.
Manager's approval is mandatory.

1. You can be paid extra day salary

OR

2. You can be given a comp off which you can use it on any other day
you prefer to take leave.
